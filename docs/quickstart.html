image‑to‑campaign.

From one product image to a complete marketing campaign — landing page, 3 Instagram Reels and a blog article, all in one consistent look.

All you need: a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cline, OpenCode …) and a Higgsfield account with credits.

1

Get a Higgsfield account

Higgsfield generates every image and video. Create your account (credits required) and log in there:

higgsfield.ai/s/mcp-arnold-oberleiter-ecZvus

2

Clone the repo & ask your agent

Tell your coding agent, word for word:

"Clone the repo https://github.com/Arnie936/image-to-campaign-en into my
skills folder and tell me what I need to do next."

The skill installs the Higgsfield CLI itself and walks you through the login — nothing to hunt down by hand.

Optional but recommended: install the Higgsfield companion skills first (better asset quality).

3

Image in → campaign out

Give the agent a product image and say:

"Turn this image into a complete campaign."

First you get a Brand Brief to approve (no credits spent yet). After your OK the agent builds everything on its own and saves it in one campaign folder.

What you get
Landing page  ·  3 Reels (9:16) + captions  ·  Blog article   — one identity, one funnel
